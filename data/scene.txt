FADE IN:

INT. A WEATHERED SUBURBAN HOME, LIVING ROOM - NIGHT
The house is dimly lit by many lamps. Photographs decorate
the walls: family portraits along with amateur stills of
varying quality. Mismatched furniture crowds the living
spaces, and the hardwood floor is littered with scuff marks.
KABIR (39), well put together in a shirt and trousers, ears
pierced with silver studs, sits in a chair in the corner with
a drink. He squints as he adjusts a table lamp next to him to
cast an appetizing glow over his whiskey.
Beside Kabir is his brother VIHAAN (34), slouched, wearing a
hoodie and cargo pants. Vihaan is peeling the label off a
beer bottle.
They watch the center of the living room.
MOHAN (69), Kabir's father, gruff, dressed in flowing
loungewear, mashes buttons on a video game controller. He is
sunk into a recliner barely held together by duct tape.
VEDANT (12), Kabir's son, sits on the floor next to Mohan.
They are playing a game against each other on the TV.

KABIR
(gesturing towards the
recliner)
Where was this Dad when we were
kids?

VIHAAN

Couldn't tell you; I was out
drinking.

Kabir SIGHS and takes a long sip of his drink.

KABIR

Not one normal conversation.

Kabir's hand floats back to the lamp, making subtle changes
to its configuration. Vihaan notices Kabir fiddling with the
lamp.

VIHAAN

Relax, Picasso, the lighting is
fine.

Kabir promptly retracts his hand, placing it on his lap,
embarrassed.
